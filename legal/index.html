Privacy

This website may collect basic enquiry information submitted through the case-assessment form, including contact details, relationship context, nationality, destination and a short case summary. The initial website does not request passport scans, national identity numbers or banking credentials.

Purpose

Submitted information may be used to respond to enquiries, assess service scope, communicate with the client and protect the website.

SimpleX

If you choose to communicate through SimpleX, those communications are subject to the technical operation of that platform and your own device/account settings.

Review note

This is starter website text and should be legally reviewed once the final operating entity, data controller, storage workflow and payment process are confirmed.

Terms

Website content is general information and does not itself create a client relationship. A paid engagement should begin only after service scope, fees, included official fee treatment, exclusions, payment terms and any cancellation/refund terms are confirmed in writing.

Genuine relationships

Viarela services are intended for genuine, consensual and lawful relationships. Viarela does not arrange .

No government affiliation

Viarela is not a governmental authority and does not decide visa, residence, immigration or civil-status outcomes.

Disclaimer

Visa, residence, immigration and civil-status decisions are made exclusively by the competent authorities. Viarela does not guarantee approval or processing time. Rules and official fees can change, and destination pages describe Viarela's commercial package structure and general service approach, not definitive legal eligibility.

Professional representation

Where regulated legal representation is required, clients may need an appropriately licensed professional in the relevant jurisdiction.

Fees & refunds

Current website package structure: Signature (€15,000), Continental (€10,000), Horizon (€7,500) and Unity Netherlands (€20,000).

Included

Consultancy, family reunification support and the standard official visa/application fee, subject to the written engagement agreement.

Excluded

Passport, transportation/travel and personal spending money are excluded. Additional translations, legal representation, appeals or third-party costs should be handled as specified in the final engagement agreement.

Settlement

After the written engagement, service fees may be settled through a designated USDT TRC20 transfer, using the payment desk instructions. Work starts only after the contracted amount is confirmed as received. Transfers sent to the wrong network or address may be irreversible.

Refund note

No refund after service commencement except where required by applicable law; statutory consumer rights remain unaffected.
