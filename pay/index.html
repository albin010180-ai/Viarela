Payment desk

Like a bank transfer. One invoice, done.

The full package fee is settled in a single Monero (XMR) payment. You do not need to understand crypto: the desk quotes the exact amount, you send it, and confirmation is automatic.

When is payment due? Case summary via SimpleX → written engagement signed → only the contracted amount is settled here. This page is not an open checkout.
1

Create the invoice

Pick your package on the card. The desk quotes the full package fee in Monero (XMR) at the live rate. The invoice is valid for roughly 30 minutes.

2

Send the exact amount

Open any Monero wallet, paste the invoice address and send the exact XMR amount shown. A QR code is there as well. No network choice: Monero has one network.

3

Confirmation is automatic

Your transfer is verified on the Monero blockchain. The invoice turns into “Payment credited”, your box updates on SimpleX — no receipt needed.

One-line translation: Address = IBAN. XMR = currency. QR = barcode. Send only on the Monero network. That is the whole system.
