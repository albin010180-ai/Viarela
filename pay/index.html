Payment desk

One invoice. Card or Monero.

Your full package fee is settled in one payment — by credit card or directly in Monero. Either way, the funds land in Viarela's Monero wallet and confirmation is automatic.

When is payment due? Case summary via SimpleX → written engagement signed → only the contracted amount is settled here. This page is not an open checkout.
1

Create the invoice

Pick your package and method on the card. Card payments are processed by our card partner; Monero is direct and fee-free. Both routes quote one exact amount for the full package fee.

2

Complete the payment

Card: use the secure checkout button and pay with Visa or Mastercard. Monero: paste the invoice address and send the exact XMR amount from any Monero wallet. Both settle into our Monero wallet.

3

Confirmation is automatic

Your payment is verified on arrival. The invoice turns into “Payment complete”, your box updates on SimpleX — no receipt needed.

One-line translation: Address = IBAN. XMR = currency. QR = barcode. Your card is handled by our payment partner. That is the whole system.
